01 · Problem statement

The Problem

Small businesses have scattered data — customer info in one place, tasks in another, sales records in Excel, reports separate, team work separate. Because of this, business owners cannot easily understand the overall business situation from one place.

"Business operations are scattered across different tools, making it difficult to manage and understand the business as a whole."

01

Customer information

Stored in phone contacts or notebook pages — easily misplaced when team members rotate.

02

Tasks & follow-ups

Tracked in chat messages that scroll past and get forgotten without accountability.

03

Sales & Excel sheets

Logged across standalone spreadsheets with no central synchronization to inventory or client status.

04

Disconnected reports

Compiled manually at the end of the month, offering hindsight rather than proactive operational insight.

02 · Proposed solution

Our Solution — OmniOS

OmniOS is a single platform that brings important business operations together — Customers, Tasks, Orders, Team, Sales, Analytics — into one dashboard, so the business owner can open OmniOS and see what's happening in the business at a glance.

Instead of jumping between disconnected software, OmniOS establishes a unified data flow where new customers automatically connect to orders, follow-up tasks, and financial summaries with zero manual duplication.

04 · Core features (MVP)

Core Features (MVP)

Thirteen planned capabilities for the complete OmniOS product, with the current review focused on the core operational prototype.

Module 01

AI Business Assistant

Contextual query interface for answering metrics and operational questions.

Module 02

CRM & Accounts

Client directory, contact histories, deal stages, and interaction logs.

Module 03

Project Management

Milestone planning, deliverable tracking, and overall project status.

Module 04

Task Kanban

Priority-ranked task assignments with due dates and interactive drag-and-drop.

Module 05

Team Workspace

Shared member directory, role designations, and departmental coordination.

Module 06

Document Management

Structured repository for invoices, client agreements, and operational files.

Module 07

Calendar & Deadlines

Integrated scheduling for follow-ups, deliveries, and milestone dates.

Module 08

Notes & Scratchpad

Quick workspace for meeting minutes, internal memos, and action lists.

Module 09

Finance Dashboard

Income, expense tracking, invoice statuses, and cash balance summaries.

Module 10

Analytics & BI

Weekly sales velocity, customer acquisition numbers, and performance curves.

Module 11

Workflow Automation

Rule-based trigger chains linking new customer intake to follow-up tasks and alerts.

Module 12

System Notifications

Real-time system alerts for overdue tasks, new orders, and impending deadlines.

Module 13

User & Role Management

Role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Staff) ensuring sensitive sales data remains accessible only to authorized personnel.
