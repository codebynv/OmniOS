01 · Problem Statement

The Problem

Small businesses have scattered data — customer information, tasks, sales records, reports and team work often live in different places. This makes it difficult to understand the overall business situation from one place.

01

Customer information

Stored across contacts, notebooks or disconnected systems.

02

Tasks & follow-ups

Tracked in chats and messages that can easily be forgotten.

03

Sales & Excel

Standalone sheets provide no unified operational picture.

04

Disconnected reports

Manual reports provide hindsight instead of timely insight.

02 · Proposed Solution

Our Solution — OmniOS

A single platform that brings Customers, Tasks, Orders, Team, Sales and Analytics into one dashboard so an owner can see what is happening at a glance.

04 · Core Features (MVP)

Core Features

The complete product roadmap contains operational modules covering CRM, project work, tasks, team coordination, documents, finance, analytics, automation and access control.

AI Business Assistant

Contextual operational questions and insights.

CRM & Accounts

Customer profiles, contact history and pipeline.

Project Management

Milestones, deliverables and project status.

Task Kanban

Priority-ranked tasks with interactive workflow.

Team Workspace

Members, roles and departmental coordination.

Document Management

Repository for operational documents and invoices.

Calendar & Deadlines

Follow-ups, deliveries and milestones.

Finance Dashboard

Income, expenses, invoices and cash summaries.

Analytics & BI

Sales, acquisition and performance summaries.
